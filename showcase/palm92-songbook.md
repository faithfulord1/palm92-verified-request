# Palm92 Songbook: six songs for safety, learning, and community

A connected set of original song concepts for Palm92 ScamShield and Faith's community work. Each song uses the requested uplifting, danceable Congolese-inspired Afrobeat / Congolese rumba palette, with a distinct story and hook. These are lyrics and generation prompts, not audio files or deployed product features.

## Shared Suno sound

Use this base prompt for all six tracks, then add the track-specific direction below:

Uplifting Congolese-inspired Afrobeat and modern Congolese rumba, 108 BPM, G major, driving danceable groove, powerful warm female lead vocal, rich layered harmonies, call-and-response chorus, dynamic build from intimate opening to anthemic climax, organic djembe and talking drum, lively sebene-inspired lead guitar, rhythmic clean guitar stabs, warm melodic bass, bright brass accents, cinematic strings, joyful community energy, full warm polished production. Keep the lyrics clear and singable. Use English lyrics with no invented foreign-language phrases. Celebrate Congolese musical influence respectfully; do not imitate a named artist.

## 1. Community safety — “Before the Money Moves”

**Use:** Palm92 ScamShield workshops and short awareness videos.  
**Scene:** Neighbours sharing a safety lesson in a bright Kinshasa courtyard; friendly, alert, never frightening.  
**Hook:** “Before the money moves, let the truth come through.”

**Suno direction:** Begin with a soft talking-drum question and one guitar phrase. Add a lively courtyard call-and-response, handclaps, and a short sebene-style guitar lift before the final chorus. Keep “check through an official channel” clear.

**Lyrics**

[Intro — spoken call]
Caller: A message says, “Pay right now!”
All: We pause first!

[Verse 1]
A bright little message comes rushing through,
“Send it today, there’s no time to lose.”
I take one breath, I don’t tap or reply,
I check who sent it from a route I know is right.

[Pre-Chorus]
Save what arrived, keep the time and the name,
Find the real source; don’t follow the frame.
If facts don’t match, let the question stay:
No rush can take my good sense away.

[Chorus]
Before the money moves, let the truth come through,
Stop, check, verify — then decide what to do.
Ask through the number or website you know,
If the facts aren’t clear, the money won’t go.
Stop! (Stop!) Check! (Check!)
Verify — then decide what’s next!

[Verse 2]
A logo can shine and a screenshot look neat,
Still check the request at its source, not the screen.
If something feels wrong, I keep every clue,
I report what I found and let people review.

[Bridge — build]
One more check, one more light,
Keep your records, make it right.
No shame in pausing, no prize for the race,
Good sense and good evidence keep us safe.

[Final Chorus]
Before the money moves, let the truth come through,
Stop, check, verify — then decide what to do.
Ask through the number or website you know,
If the facts aren’t clear, the money won’t go.

## 2. Employment support — “My Next Chapter”

**Use:** Women returning to work, confidence workshops, and interview-practice sessions.  
**Scene:** Women getting ready together before a new day; supportive, confident, and joyful.  
**Hook:** “My next chapter, my own name.”

**Suno direction:** Start intimate with Rhodes and a close vocal, then bring in warm guitar, handclaps, harmonies, and a celebratory dance groove. Keep the mood encouraging and grounded, not a promise of a job.

**Lyrics**

[Verse 1]
I’ve carried a season, I’ve learned on the way,
I’ve solved real problems day after day.
The care, the courage, the plans I made,
They’re part of the strength that I bring to the page.

[Pre-Chorus]
I know what I’ve done; I can tell it true,
I can name my skills and the value I bring through.
One clear example, one answer at a time,
I’m ready to open a door that is mine.

[Chorus]
My next chapter, my own name,
I bring my whole life, I bring my flame.
Learn it, practise, walk in tall,
I have a place, I can answer the call.
My next chapter, step by step,
My voice, my skill, my promise kept.

[Verse 2]
I practise my story, I ask what I need,
I make a small plan and I plant the seed.
A new beginning does not erase
The strength I built in a different place.

[Bridge — call and response]
Who knows her worth? (I know my worth!)
Who brings her skill? (I bring my skill!)
One step forward, the future is wide,
We lift each other; we rise with pride.

[Final Chorus]
My next chapter, my own name,
I bring my whole life, I bring my flame.
Learn it, practise, walk in tall,
I have a place, I can answer the call.

## 3. Cybersecurity and rail learning — “Know Your Role”

**Use:** Memory support for learning sessions; not a substitute for current railway rules, an approved briefing, or an instructor.  
**Scene:** A learner reviewing, asking questions, and checking before acting.  
**Hook:** “Pause, check the brief — know your role.”

**Suno direction:** Use crisp guitar stabs like a steady count, a clear rhythm for the memory words, and a strong group chorus. Keep the lyric general: no operational instructions or claims that one mnemonic covers every worksite.

**Lyrics**

[Verse 1 — cyber]
A message comes in with a link to pursue,
I stop and check who is asking me to.
The sender, the context, the channel, the clue,
I verify first; I don’t pass it through.

[Pre-Chorus]
Spot it, stop it, check what is true,
Use the trusted route that is known to you.
If something is wrong, keep evidence clear,
Report it through the right channel here.

[Chorus]
Pause, check the brief — know your role,
Ask if it’s unclear before you go.
Use the current rules, the approved way,
Check, confirm, then act today.
Pause! Check! Know your role!
Safety and learning go hand in hand.

[Verse 2 — rail learning]
Before a task, I listen and learn,
I check the approved brief at each turn.
My role and protection, the boundary shown,
If I’m not certain, I stop and ask, I don’t go alone.

[Bridge — instructor and learners]
Learner: If the plan has changed?
All: Stop and confirm!
Learner: If I’m not sure?
All: Ask before acting!
Lead: Follow the current approved rules.
All: Check, confirm, then move!

[Final Chorus]
Pause, check the brief — know your role,
Ask if it’s unclear before you go.
Use the current rules, the approved way,
Check, confirm, then act today.

## 4. Church and community — “Many Hands, One Heart”

**Use:** Volunteer appreciation, community events, and workshop openers.  
**Scene:** A joyful community gathering with a warm gospel choir over Congolese guitar and percussion.  
**Hook:** “Many hands, one heart, make a welcome place.”

**Suno direction:** Begin with a small choir and gentle guitar, then build into a bright gospel-influenced call-and-response chorus, brass accents, handclaps, and a celebratory guitar break. Keep it welcoming to the whole community.

**Lyrics**

[Verse 1]
You set the table, you learn every name,
You make room for the shy and the new.
A little kindness can brighten a day,
A welcoming word can carry us through.

[Chorus]
Many hands, one heart, make a welcome place,
Many voices, one song, every neighbour has space.
We serve with joy, we show that we care,
When one person needs us, the whole community’s there.

[Verse 2]
You share what you know, you give what you can,
You notice the person who’s standing alone.
No task is too small when it’s offered with love,
Together we make every welcome feel home.

[Bridge — choir]
For every quiet hour, for every open door,
For every helping hand, we thank you more and more.
The good we give keeps travelling on,
A chorus of care that carries us strong.

[Final Chorus]
Many hands, one heart, make a welcome place,
Many voices, one song, every neighbour has space.
We serve with joy, we show that we care,
When one person needs us, the whole community’s there.

## 5. Palm92 videos — “Proof in the Rhythm” (20-second hook)

**Use:** Original musical hook for ScamShield match-cut videos.  
**Visual relay:** A hand pauses above a phone → the same gesture opens an evidence file → a magnifying glass checks an official source → a person decides to wait. Use fictional, sanitized screens only.  
**Hook:** “Stop, check, verify — let the proof lead the way.”

**Suno direction:** Generate a short 20-second cue with a strong first beat, clear vocal hook, and clean ending for an end card. Leave space for voice-over. Build one recurring guitar motif across each visual cut; end on a warm brass and choir hit.

**Lyrics**

[Hook]
Stop, check, verify — let the proof lead the way.
Palm92, people decide — make a safer move today.
Stop! Check! Verify!
Let the proof lead the way!

[End-card tag]
Palm92 ScamShield.
AI investigates. Humans decide.

## 6. Personal outreach — “A Little Thank You”

**Use:** Thank-you songs or a warm personal greeting, only with the recipient’s consent to use their details.  
**Scene:** A small celebration with friends, family, or colleagues; affectionate, playful, and respectful.  
**Hook:** “A little thank you, a melody to say.”

**Suno direction:** Keep the shared Afrobeat/Congolese rumba palette, but make this version intimate and personal: warm guitar, light percussion, tender lead vocal, soft group harmonies, then a joyful final refrain. Use only approved names and non-sensitive facts. Never invent personal details.

**Lyrics**

[Verse 1]
Today we’re gathering, bringing our cheer,
For all the kindness you’ve shared through the year.
The moments you gave, the way you showed care,
They made this small song worth bringing here.

[Chorus]
A little thank you, a melody to say,
We’re glad you’re in our lives today.
May laughter find you, may good things grow,
This song is a hug in a rhythm you know.

[Verse 2]
For every kind word and the time that you gave,
For standing beside us, for helping us brave,
We’ve wrapped up our thanks in a tune we can sing,
A bright little chorus for all that you bring.

[Final Chorus]
A little thank you, a melody to say,
We’re glad you’re in our lives today.
May laughter find you, may good things grow,
This song is a hug in a rhythm you know.
