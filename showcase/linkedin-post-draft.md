# LinkedIn post draft — Palm92 ScamShield

I’ve been exploring a practical question for Palm92 ScamShield:

How can we help people pause before sending money or sharing information when a request looks convincing?

My answer is not “let AI decide.”

Palm92’s direction is evidence first: capture and preserve the request, extract what it is asking someone to do, check the source independently, explain what is known and what remains uncertain, then keep the consequential decision with a person.

I’ve created a fictional, privacy-safe campaign poster and documented a free-first workflow playbook to make that idea easier to share. It includes practical routes for product storytelling, music, open-source tools, and personal privacy clean-up, with clear notes on what is a concept and what is not yet a live integration.

One useful lesson: open source and free tiers can lower the barrier, but “free to download” does not always mean free to operate. A video-generation skill may rely on paid credits; a self-hosted privacy tool still needs secure setup and maintenance. I want to be transparent about those trade-offs.

Palm92 ScamShield is at the hackathon MVP and market-validation stage. I’m sharing the direction and the work in progress, not claiming a finished production service.

**Don’t trust the screenshot. Verify the request.**  
**STOP → VERIFY → DECIDE.**  
**AI investigates. Humans decide.**

The public showcase, poster, and workflow playbook are here: https://github.com/faithfulord1/palm92-verified-request/pull/7
