# LinkedIn post draft — Palm92 ScamShield

I’ve been exploring a practical question for Palm92 ScamShield:

How can we help people pause before sending money or sharing information when a request looks convincing?

My answer is not “let AI decide.”

Palm92’s direction is evidence first: capture and preserve the request, extract what it is asking someone to do, check the source independently, explain what is known and what remains uncertain, then keep the consequential decision with a person.

I’ve created a fictional, privacy-safe campaign poster and documented a free-first workflow playbook to make that idea easier to share. I’ve also drafted six original song concepts: a “Check Before You Pay” jingle, encouragement for women returning to work, learning hooks for cybersecurity and rail, a volunteer appreciation song, a ScamShield video hook, and a consent-led thank-you song.

The songs share an uplifting Congolese-inspired Afrobeat and rumba direction, while each has its own story. They’re lyrics and Suno prompts at this stage, not finished audio. Suno’s current free plan has limits and no commercial-use rights, so I’m being clear about that before anyone uses generated audio publicly.

One useful lesson: open source and free tiers can lower the barrier, but “free to download” does not always mean free to operate. A video-generation skill may rely on paid credits; a self-hosted privacy tool still needs secure setup and maintenance.

Palm92 ScamShield is at the hackathon MVP and market-validation stage. I’m sharing the direction and the work in progress, not claiming a finished production service.

**Don’t trust the screenshot. Verify the request.**  
**STOP → VERIFY → DECIDE.**  
**AI investigates. Humans decide.**
