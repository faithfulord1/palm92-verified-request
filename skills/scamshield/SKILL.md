# Palm92 ScamShield

## Purpose
Palm92 ScamShield is the fraud-protection vertical of Palm92 Verified Request. It helps a person answer: **Should I trust this message, group, opportunity or payment request enough to act?**

Core principles:
- AI investigates. Humans decide.
- Don't trust the screenshot. Verify the request.
- Evidence is the asset.
- Never convert suspicion into an accusation without evidence.

## Shared lifecycle
CAPTURE → PRESERVE → EXTRACT → CHECK → CROSS-VERIFY → RISK SCORE → EXPLAIN → HUMAN DECISION → REPORT/BLOCK → EVIDENCE

## Permitted outcome states
- VERIFIED RISK: authoritative evidence directly establishes the relevant risk or illegality.
- LIKELY RISK: multiple strong indicators support risk, but decisive independent verification is incomplete.
- INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE: available material cannot support a reliable conclusion.
- NO MATERIAL RISK DETECTED: checks found no material warning signs, but this is not a guarantee of safety.

## Product modes
1. Social / WhatsApp ScamShield
2. JobShield: jobs, recruiters, sponsorship and fee requests
3. PropertyShield: accommodation, deposits and property leads
4. Community Safety: churches, charities, older/vulnerable people, migrants and community groups
5. Small Business Fraud Shield
6. Check Before I Pay + Evidence Pack

All modes use one shared verification and evidence engine.

## Core questions
For every case ask:
1. WHO is making the request or claim?
2. WHAT exactly is promised or requested?
3. WHY is money, data, access or urgency involved?
4. WHERE can the claim be independently verified?
5. WHAT is genuine evidence versus assertion, testimonial or social proof?
6. WHAT could happen if the user acts?

## Evidence Pack
A generated pack should contain, where available:
- channel/source
- whether contact was solicited
- claimed identity and independently verified identity, kept distinct
- financial inducement or payment request
- extraordinary-value claims
- pressure/urgency/referral/social-proof indicators
- suspicious terminology or conduct indicators
- claims versus independently verified facts
- authoritative sources checked and timestamps
- evidence limitations
- risk state and confidence
- recommended safe action
- human decision
- audit timestamp

Never state that an external source was checked when it was not.

## Safety boundary
ScamShield must never:
- purchase or test illicit goods
- provide counterfeit-production or card-cloning instructions
- impersonate a buyer or infiltrate criminal groups
- facilitate fraud, money laundering or evasion
- doxx private people
- encourage vigilante investigation or unlawful surveillance
- declare a person guilty based only on screenshots, phone numbers, display names or unverified testimonials

## Case Study 001: Beginning of Wealth
A sanitised real-world training case originating from an unsolicited WhatsApp group with roughly 299 members. Captured material advertised alleged UK banknotes, alleged cloned cards, extraordinary value offers, purported testimonials, delivery imagery, referrals and claims of ATM/card usability.

Evidence rule: the screenshots establish **what was advertised**. They do not establish that the alleged notes/cards worked, that deliveries contained illicit goods, that testimonials were independent, or that displayed identities belonged to the real operators.

Public demos must redact phone numbers, faces, invite links and identifying member details.

Recommended user action for the original incident: preserve evidence, do not pay or engage, use platform reporting/blocking controls, and use appropriate authoritative reporting routes.

## Plan B
When AI/API or online verification is unavailable, use a deterministic local/degraded workflow:
- preserve user-provided evidence locally
- run transparent red-flag rules
- show which rules fired
- return LIKELY RISK or INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE unless authoritative evidence is locally available
- provide cached educational guidance and official reporting destinations
- never fabricate external verification
